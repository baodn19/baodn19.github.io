I assisted this project as part of the work at the <a href="https://era-research-lab.github.io/">ERA Lab</a> with the collaboration of <a href="https://arl.devcom.army.mil/">U.S. Army DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory</a> and the <a href="https://www.usf.edu/engineering/iae/">University of South Florida's Institute of Applied Engineering (IAE)</a>. Below are my contributions.

- Evaluated retrieval capability of an RAG system with an ablation study on RAGBench’s subsets (science, legal contracts, etc).
- Measured the latency of each Dockerized microservice and identified the diarization service as the bottleneck.
- Improved diarization by 33% in accuracy and 67% in latency by migrating from Diart & Pyannote to Sortformer & Parakeet.
